# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

GryChat ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our messaging application and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Email address** (used for authentication via Firebase)
- **Display name** and **username** (provided during registration)
- **Profile picture** (optional, stored locally on your device)

### 2.2 Message Content
- **Text messages** sent and received through the application
- **Media files** (images, audio, video, documents) shared through the application
- Messages are encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL

### 2.3 Device Information
- **Device type** and operating system version
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token** for push notifications
- **IP address** (logged temporarily for connection management)

### 2.4 Usage Data
- **Connection timestamps** and session duration
- **Feature usage** patterns (anonymized)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Core Functionality
- To provide messaging services between users
- To deliver push notifications for new messages and calls
- To maintain user presence and online status
- To enable voice and video calling features

### 3.2 Security and Performance
- To authenticate users and prevent unauthorized access
- To monitor and improve application performance
- To detect and prevent abuse or security threats

### 3.3 Communication
- To send service-related notifications (e.g., security updates)
- To respond to user support requests

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Location
- **Messages** are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security
- **Authentication** is managed by Firebase Authentication
- **Local data** is stored on your device using Hive (encrypted local database)

### 4.2 Security Measures
- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
- Row-level security policies ensure users can only access their own data
- Firebase Admin SDK enforces server-side authentication
- Certificate pinning prevents man-in-the-middle attacks

### 4.3 Data Retention
- Messages are retained as long as your account is active
- You can delete individual messages or clear conversation history
- Account deletion removes your data from our servers within 30 days

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

### 5.2 Service Providers
We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating our application:
- **Firebase** (Google) - Authentication and crash reporting
- **Supabase** - Database hosting and storage

### 5.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## 6. Your Rights

### 6.1 Access and Control
- **View** your profile information at any time
- **Update** your display name and profile picture
- **Delete** individual messages or entire conversations
- **Export** your data upon request

### 6.2 Account Deletion
You can request account deletion by contacting us. Upon deletion:
- Your profile information is removed from our database
- Your messages are anonymized or deleted
- Your FCM token is deregistered

### 6.3 Opt-Out
- You can disable push notifications in your device settings
- You can delete your account at any time

## 7. Children's Privacy

GryChat is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy in the application and updating the "Last Updated" date.
